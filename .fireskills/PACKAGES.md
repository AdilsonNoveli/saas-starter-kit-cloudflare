No new distinct row above: same package/version already inventoried for `apps/functions`, workspace-deduplicated by `$fireskill-documentator`. `bun install` run; `bun.lock` updated. **Extended the same day, twice more:** `layers/admin/package.json` and `layers/notifications/package.json` gained the identical `drizzle-kit` devDependency first (one-directional-or-no FK shape against `core-schema.ts`, verified before touching anything); then, once `Job`/`DeadLetterRecord` moved out of `core-schema.ts` into `layers/teams/server/database/schema.ts` to break its bidirectional FK cycle with base, `layers/teams/package.json` gained the same `drizzle-kit` devDependency plus a `generate-migrations` script, matching every other layer with its own history. Same dedup, same `bun install` each time.
- **SPEC-0008 (2026-09-30):** `@iconify-json/ph` (MIT) replaces `@iconify-json/lucide` in `apps/app`, `apps/login` and `apps/web`; `@nuxt/fonts` 0.10.3 is now also declared in `apps/login`; `@nuxfire/tokens` is now a dependency of `packages/emails`. The fonts Atkinson Hyperlegible and IBM Plex Mono (OFL-1.1) add no package: the `fontsource` provider of `@nuxt/fonts` downloads them at build time and serves them from the app's own domain.
- **SPEC-0009 (2026-10-06):** `@nuxfire/crypto` (`packages/crypto`) and `@nuxfire/rate-limit` (`packages/rate-limit`) are workspace packages with no external origin: their code was moved out of this repository, not copied from a third party. `packages/crypto` gathers the AES-GCM helpers that lived in the app's `mfa-crypto.ts`, the pure half of its `mfa-recovery.ts`, its `ws-ticket.ts`, and the HMAC-SHA256 that `apps/functions/src/websockets.js` and `layers/billing/server/utils/billing/paddle-team-signature.ts` each wrote by hand; `packages/rate-limit` gathers the fixed-window algorithm of `apps/app/server/utils/rate-limit.ts` and `apps/functions/src/rate-limit.ts`, which both remain as thin wrappers over it. Neither declares a runtime dependency; `@nuxfire/layer-account` (`layers/account`, same spec) depends on both. Responsibilities: `.fireskills/STACK.md`.
