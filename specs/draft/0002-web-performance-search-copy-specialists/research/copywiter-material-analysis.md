## Provenance and rights (must be settled by the owner)

- `alta-conversão.md` and `copy-d9.md` contain a URL of an affiliate programme
  (`desafio19dias.com`) and scripts to promote that programme. This is promotional material for a
  third party's product, normally licensed for that promotion only.
- Every component file opens with an instruction to the buyer of the kit ("you may fill the blanks
  according to your niche"). The material is a bought or distributed template kit.
- Whether the owner may redistribute it, adapt it or ship derivatives inside a product sold to
  others is **unknown to this analysis**. Consequence for the design: the raw material stays out of
  the repository that is delivered to buyers (DEC-006), and the specialist stores **original
  structural knowledge** written in the project's own words.

## Extraction method (task T050)

1. Read all 37 files, one pass, no summarising by tool.
2. Build a table of **patterns**: name, mental trigger, structure (slots, in the project's own
   words), typical position (headline, opening, close), the awareness stage it suits (C6), a
   compliance class and the reason.
3. Compliance classes: **A** no factual claim is needed; **B** needs a ledger entry (a number, a
   result, a comparison, a guarantee, a testimonial); **C** forbidden (invented proof, fake
   urgency, exploitation of fear, income or health promises without evidence, guilt).
4. Deduplicate; expected result 15-30 distinct patterns from about 2,600 items (an estimate to be
   replaced by the real count).
5. For each retained pattern write two original examples for a developer-tool page in en, pt-br and
   es, each tied to a claim that exists in the ledger or to no claim.
6. Reject verbatim reuse: a test compares the published reference against the raw files and fails
   on any run of 8 or more consecutive identical words (threshold to be confirmed in T050).
