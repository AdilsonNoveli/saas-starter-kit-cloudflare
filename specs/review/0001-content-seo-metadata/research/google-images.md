# Research note: Google Search supported image formats

- **Source**: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/google-images
- **Retrieved**: 2026-09-25 (fetched through the documentation tool of this session; the page text was summarized by the tool, not copied)
- **License / use**: public documentation; this note paraphrases and quotes only short statements as provenance.

## Statements relied on

- Images referenced by `img` `src` are supported in BMP, GIF, JPEG, PNG, WebP, SVG and AVIF.
- **Impact**: a PNG cover satisfies Google and Facebook; SVG alone would not satisfy Facebook (R-002, R-003).
